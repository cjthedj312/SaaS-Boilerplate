# The Objection Crusher Matrix

### Objection 1: "I already have voicemail."
* **The Reality:** 80%+ of customers who reach a contractor's voicemail immediately hang up and call the next contractor in Google Maps.
* **The Rebuttal:**  
  > *"Totally get that, [Name]. But when was the last time YOU left a voicemail and waited around when you needed something done fast? People want instant gratification. The second they hang up, our system texts them: 'Sorry I missed you, tap here to pick a time.' That one text saves \$1,000s in lost work."*

### Objection 2: "I don't have budget right now / sounds expensive."
* **The Rebuttal:**  
  > *"What's your average ticket on a job? \$300? \$600? If this system catches just ONE missed call this month that would have called your competitor, it already paid for itself 3 times over. It's not an expense—it's a digital safety net for your money."*

### Objection 3: "I'm not tech savvy."
* **The Rebuttal:**  
  > *"You don't touch any tech at all. We configure everything on our end. You just keep answering your phone like you always do. If you miss a call, the system handles it in the background automatically."*
