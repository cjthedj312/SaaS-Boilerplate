# 15-Minute Deal Closer Script

### Phase 1: Context & The Math (0–3 Min)
> *"Hey [Name], thanks for jumping on. Let's get straight to the point so you can get back to work. You mentioned you miss about 3 to 5 calls a week when you're on a job or driving. If an average job is \$400, that's \$1,200 to \$2,000 in revenue walking out the door every week to competitors."*

### Phase 2: The Live Live Test (3–8 Min)
> *"I'm going to call the test line right now from my cell. Watch what happens when I hang up without leaving a voicemail."*  
*(Trigger missed call -> Contractor's phone receives instant text with booking link within 5 seconds)*  
> *"See that? If a homeowner called you right now, instead of feeling ignored, they're already booking a time slot on your calendar."*

### Phase 3: The Close (8–15 Min)
> *"We have two ways to roll this out:  
1. **The Flash Setup (\$97 one-time):** We plug the missed-call auto-text into your current number, test it, and hand you the keys.  
2. **The Full Agency Growth (\$397/mo):** We handle full AI booking, review generation, and Google profile optimization.  
Which makes more sense for your business today?"*
