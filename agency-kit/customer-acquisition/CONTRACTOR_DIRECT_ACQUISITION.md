## Why Craigslist Services Is the Fastest Channel on Earth
* Contractors pay \$5 to list their services on Craigslist under **Services > Skilled Trades** or **Labor / Gigs**.
* They list their **direct personal cell phone numbers** on the ad.
* They are actively staring at their phone waiting for job leads today.

---

## Step-by-Step Acquisition Protocol

### Step 1: Find 10 Active Ads (Takes 5 minutes)
1. Go to `https://cosprings.craigslist.org` (or Denver, or your target city).
2. Click **Services** -> **Skilled Trade** or **Household Services**.
3. Search for:
   - `gutter cleaning`
   - `pressure washing`
   - `window washing`
   - `handyman`
   - `roofing`
4. Pick 10 listings that include a cell phone number.

### Step 2: The Mystery Call (Takes 3 minutes)
1. Call the number from your phone during working hours (11:00 AM – 3:00 PM).
2. If they answer: Say *"Hey! Just checking if you guys handle residential jobs in the area. I actually build automated booking systems for contractors—do you ever miss calls while working on roofs?"*
3. If they **don't answer** (or it goes to voicemail): **Perfect! Hang up immediately.**

### Step 3: Send the Mystery Call Follow-Up SMS (Highest Conversion)
Send this exact text immediately:

> *"Hey [Name], tried calling your shop just now to ask about a job, but got your voicemail. Most homeowners hang up and call the next guy on Google instead of leaving a message.*
>
> *We install an auto-text on your business line for a flat \$97 that texts callers an instant booking link in 5 seconds so you never lose a \$400 job.*
>
> *I can set it up on your phone line today. Want me to send the checkout link?"*
